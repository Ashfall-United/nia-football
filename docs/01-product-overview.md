# Nia Football
## Product Overview

**Positioning:** A football operating system that captures the game at source and turns it into usable intelligence.

**Vision:** The operating system for football development in Africa.

---

## 1. Product

Nia Football is a multi-tenant football video and intelligence platform for clubs, academies, competitions, coaches, analysts and media teams.

Nia begins at the pitch. It lets football organisations capture matches and training sessions, livestream them, store the footage, create clips, tag football events and progressively turn video into structured football intelligence.

The product is designed for African football environments first. It must work with smartphones, modest equipment, mixed pitch surfaces and unreliable connectivity.

Nia is not a generic video platform with football labels. The product model, workflows and analytics are built around football operations and player development.

---

## 2. Core Promise

**Capture → Store → Analyse → Understand → Develop**

The first product must solve a simple problem extremely well:

> A team can arrive at a pitch, record or livestream a session, and have the footage safely available in its Nia workspace without having to manage video files manually.

The platform then turns that footage into increasingly useful football information.

---

## 3. Primary Users

### Media Team

Responsible for recording and livestreaming.

Needs:

- Fast camera setup
- Phone-first recording
- Clear stream status
- Camera assignment
- Live monitoring
- Multiple camera support
- Reliable operation on weak connections

### Coach

Uses footage to review team performance and communicate with players.

Needs:

- Recent sessions and matches
- Fast playback
- Timeline navigation
- Clips
- Notes
- Player tagging
- Playlists
- Sharing

### Analyst

Turns footage into structured information.

Needs:

- Event tagging
- Player tagging
- Clip creation
- Event timelines
- Filters
- Match/session comparison
- Data export
- Future AI-assisted tagging

### Organisation Administrator

Manages the club or academy workspace.

Needs:

- Teams
- Users
- Roles
- Subscription
- Storage and usage
- Organisation settings
- Permissions
