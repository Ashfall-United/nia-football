# Nia Football

## The operating system for football development in Africa.

### A football operating system that captures the game at source and turns it into usable intelligence.

Football organisations already have cameras, phones, coaches, analysts and players.

The problem is that the game is often scattered across phones, WhatsApp messages, hard drives, social media accounts and disconnected spreadsheets.

**Nia brings the football workflow into one place.**

---

## Capture

Record matches and training sessions directly from the pitch using a phone or compatible camera.

You can start with one camera and expand to multiple camera angles as your operation grows.

---

## Livestream

Broadcast matches and sessions online.

Use a single camera or multiple camera sources depending on your setup.

---

## Store

Your football footage is stored in the cloud.

No more filling phones and laptops with full match recordings.

Keep important clips and football assets for the long term.

---

## Analyse

Coaches and analysts can:

- Watch full matches and training sessions
- Create clips
- Tag players
- Tag football events
- Add notes
- Build playlists
- Review sessions
- Build a searchable video history

---

## Build Football Intelligence

Nia is being built to progressively turn video into structured football information.

The platform will develop AI-assisted capabilities for:

- Player detection
- Ball detection
- Player tracking
- Event suggestions
- Automatic highlight suggestions
- Player analysis

And eventually:

**Structure. Movement. Space. Connection. Pressing. Transitions.**

---

# Built for African Football

Nia is designed around the conditions teams actually face.

It can support football played on:

- Grass
- Turf
- Gravel
- Sand
- Mud

The system is designed around mobile capture, limited bandwidth and unreliable connectivity.

You do not need an expensive media department or specialist camera system to start.
